Garland math

The mantle is 5 meters across, the garland comes in fixed strands, and nobody says how much the swag eats. Garland math adds the droop, rounds up to real strands, counts the greenery bunches and the florist wire, and works out the lights. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

The three tools

  • Garland plan - run length and swag droop become garland meters and labeled strands with the spare.
  • Bunch plan - garland length and spacing become greenery bunches and florist wire.
  • Light plan - garland length and bulb density become bulbs, strands and spares.

Labeled norms

Labeled strand length: 2.7 m - always round up. Labeled swag droop allowance: about 10-30% over the straight run depending on how deep the swags hang. Labeled bunch spacing: about 20 cm, one extra bunch for the end. Labeled florist wire: about 30 cm a bunch. Labeled lights: about 20 bulbs a meter, sold in 100-bulb strands. These are reference values, not rules - fresh greenery packs looser than artificial, so measure a bunch when it matters.
